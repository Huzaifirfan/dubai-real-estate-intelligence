Charts include relevant existing measures in tooltips. All sales measures retain
GROUP_EN = "Sales". Price measures retain VALID_SALE_PRICE_METRIC = 1 and the Sales
filter. Shares and growth use DIVIDE. No Total Sales Value, Total Market Value,
forecast, investment score or SUM(TRANS_VALUE) market KPI exists.

Time interpretation:
With no date selection, Sales Records is YTD. Previous Month Sales Records is
August 2026 (the full prior calendar month), and MoM Sales Record Growth % compares
partial September through 21 September with full August. The warning and page
footer disclose this unequal period length; it is not a forecast or same-day
comparison. Selecting a month in a chart changes the relevant date context.

Sorting support is Power Query metadata, not new DAX measures:
TRANSACTION_VALUE_BAND_SORT (hidden Int64, values 1–6)
PROPERTY_SIZE_BAND_SORT (hidden Int64, values 1–5)
Original band labels, spaces and case are unchanged. Unexpected future labels
sort at 99 rather than being replaced or silently discarded.

The model uses Latin1_General_100_CS_AS collation to preserve case-only source
area variants as distinct values. This is necessary for Stage 11 area fidelity.
